Journal comparison — who reads what

Who reads a work differently?

Pick a work, then two sets of journals. The chart shows, for each passage or book, how much more one set's citations concentrate on it than the other's — a positive bar where Set A leans in, a negative bar where Set B does. Shares are computed within the period you select, so this is emphasis, not raw volume.
